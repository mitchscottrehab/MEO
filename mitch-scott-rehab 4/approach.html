SOUND FAMILIAR?

You're doing everything right.
It's still not working.

It might not be a you problem, it might be the system you're stuck in.

You've rested it. Seen someone about it. It's still there. Every unanswered question costs you another week of training, another event crossed off the calendar. You don't need more rest. You need better answers.

You've probably heard...

01

"Just rest it and see how it feels in a few weeks."

So you did. You gave it weeks, then eased back in carefully. But it flared anyway, because rest was never a plan, it was just the absence of one.

02

"You should probably stop training for a while."

No modified plan. No "here's what you can still do." Just an open-ended stop, leaving you to figure out the rest for yourself.

03

"Try these stretches, they might help."

Three exercises you could've found on the first page of Google, with no explanation of why they were chosen or what they were meant to fix.

04

"We're basically out of time, same again next week?"

A 20 minute slot was never enough to get to the bottom of anything, no matter how good the intent behind it.

At MEO, every plan is built from three disciplines: Physiotherapy, Exercise Physiology, and Sports Science. Not because it sounds impressive. Because each one answers a different question.

Physiotherapy helps find the source, severity and symptom profile. Exercise Physiology helps rebuild the capacity. Sports Science makes sure the transition from rehab to performance is seamless. The goal isn't to just return, it's to return better than you left. And every plan is made to match what your training actually demands from your body, no matter the stage you're at.

Most people think they need to pick one. MEO uses all three. Broad view, narrow focus.

Mitch Scott
Physiotherapist · Exercise Physiologist · Sports Scientist · AHPRA Registered

Mitch holds a Master of Physiotherapy Studies from the University of Queensland, and a Bachelor of Clinical Exercise Physiology (1st Class Honours) with a background in Exercise & Sports Science. That combination is what lets a session move past "does it still hurt?" into "can your body actually handle what you're asking of it?". That is the real question that actually determines whether you flare up again.

HOW IT PLAYS OUT IN EVERY SESSION

01

Extended sessions

Enough time to actually assess the whole picture, not just treat where it hurts, but where it started. The source and the site of pain aren't always the same place.

02

A performance target, not a symptom target

Your first session ends with a specific, measurable answer to "what does success look like?" Not "less pain." Something specific. Something that means something to you.

03

Training through, not around

Where possible, you keep training. Modified, structured, purposeful, but you remain active throughout the entire process. Stopping isn't rehab. It's just stopping.

04

A plan that ends at performance, not pain-free

Most rehab ends at pain-free. Ours doesn't. Let's set a target and get to work.
