THE DEEPER TRUTH

Pain-free isn't the finish line.

Here's what nobody tells you... pain doesn't mean damage. And the absence of pain doesn't mean you're ready.

Your body is always asking one question: can you handle this? Not today, moving carefully in a controlled room. At full speed. Under load. In the exact patterns your sport demands.

Most rehab stops the moment pain does. So you return to training with less capacity than you think. For movements you haven't prepared for.

So you come back. Three sessions in, it flares. Not because you're unlucky. Not because your body is broken. Because nobody built you back up to what your sport/activity actually asks of you.

You've been treating the symptom. Nobody has treated you.

MEO is a boutique performance rehabilitation practice. That means:

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Still deciding?
The honest answers.

Does online rehab actually work?

Yes. Online rehab follows the same principles as in-person: thorough assessment, an individualised plan, and regular check-ins, with the same attention to detail.

How is this different from an app or a generic PDF of exercises?

This is bespoke rehab and coaching from a practitioner, not a one-and-done plan you're handed and left to run. It's adjusted as you go, so you get the most out of every week.

What's the difference between an Initial and a Subsequent consultation?

Your Initial consultation covers a full assessment of your history, movement and goals, and sets out a plan. Subsequent consultations build on that plan: hands-on rehab, coaching, or progress checks, depending on where you're at.

How do I choose between in-person, online, or the full package?

If you're in Brisbane, and want face-to-face rehab, go in-person. If you're remote or time-poor, online covers the same ground. If you want ongoing support and programming over 12 weeks, the Peak Performance package is built for you.

Can I claim these sessions with my private health fund?

Yes, face-to-face sessions are claimable under Physiotherapy cover (pending your individual policy). For online/telehealth consultations, we recommend checking with your specific health fund.
