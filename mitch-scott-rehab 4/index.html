THE MAN BEHIND MEO

Hi, I'm Mitch.

Physiotherapist, Exercise Physiologist &
Sports Scientist

The rehab industry moves fast and cuts corners, and the athlete often pays the price. I built MEO to do the opposite. To slow down, find the source, and walk beside you every step of the way until you're back doing what you love, without hesitation or fear. The devil is in the details, and the best plan in the world is worthless if it doesn't work for the person doing it. MEO exists to build every plan from the ground up, without the rush, to keep you moving forward.

I graduated from the University of Queensland with a Masters of Physiotherapy Studies, following a Bachelor of Clinical Exercise Physiology (1st Class Honours). Since then I've worked with weekend warriors, gym goers, and everyday people who simply love to train, alongside athletes from QAFL and NPL clubs. My background as an Exercise Physiologist helps me blend traditional physiotherapy with a refined exercise toolkit, so you get a well-rounded plan. The priority is always the same: to guide you through rehab safely and efficiently, rebuild real confidence in your body, and get you back to training without overthinking every movement.

When I'm not in the clinic, you'll usually find me training myself, hunting down Brisbane's best pastries, or heading to the beach for a weekend away (when possible...).

THE MODEL

This isn't a 20-minute appointment
and a theraband.

MEO is a boutique performance rehabilitation practice. That means:

01

Extended sessions

Enough time to actually assess the whole picture, not just treat where it hurts, but where it started. The source and the site of pain aren't always the same place.

02

A performance target, not a symptom target

Your first session ends with a specific, measurable answer to "what does success look like?" Not "less pain." Something specific. Something that means something to you.

03

Training through, not around

Where possible, you keep training. Modified, structured, purposeful, but you remain active throughout the entire process. Stopping isn't rehab. It's just stopping.

04

A plan that ends at performance, not pain-free

Most rehab ends after Phase 1. Ours doesn't.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Got questions?
Here's the truth.

Does online rehab actually work?

Yes. Online rehab follows the same principles as in-person: thorough assessment, an individualised plan, and regular check-ins, with the same attention to detail.

How is this different from an app or a generic PDF of exercises?

This is bespoke rehab and coaching from a practitioner, not a one-and-done plan you're handed and left to run. It's adjusted as you go, so you get the most out of every week.

What's the difference between an Initial and a Subsequent consultation?

Your Initial consultation covers a full assessment of your history, movement and goals, and sets out a plan. Subsequent consultations build on that plan: hands-on rehab, coaching, or progress checks, depending on where you're at.

How do I choose between in-person, online, or the full package?

If you're in Brisbane, and want face-to-face rehab, go in-person. If you're remote or time-poor, online covers the same ground. If you want ongoing support and programming over 12 weeks, the Peak Performance package is built for you.

Can I claim these sessions with my private health fund?

Yes, face-to-face sessions are claimable under Physiotherapy cover (pending your individual policy). For online/telehealth consultations, we recommend checking with your specific health fund.
