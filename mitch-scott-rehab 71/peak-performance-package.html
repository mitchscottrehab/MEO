MOMNTUM.

Rehab built around your training, so you keep moving while we work on the injury.

Momentum /mə-ˈmen-təm/ • noun

1. Mass in motion. The force a moving object carries. Once it's moving, it tends to keep moving.

2. The drive to keep going. Hard to build from a standstill, and easier to keep than to get back.

The program

Different Options for Different Paces.

Everything else stays the same across all three. I'll recommend the one that fits at your assessment, based on what you need.

Training is who you are.
Don't let an injury change that.

Most rehab is built to settle pain, so the default is to stop training. Momentum is built the other way round. Your training stays in the plan while the injury settles, and the plan keeps building once it has, toward what your training actually asks of you.

01

02

03

Build toward more.

Heavier, faster, closer to what your sport asks of you.

How it works

What the first few months look like.

Week 1

It starts with an assessment

On the gym floor or online, not a treatment bed. We go through what you've tried, where you're at now and where you're trying to get to. From there we set clear targets and build a plan that works back from the end goal, phase by phase.

Your plan

Rehab and training in one plan

Your training stays in the plan, in the MEO app. Anything that can't stay gets modified, not deleted.

Monday to Friday

Full access to me during the week

If something's off, the plan changes then and there, not at your next session. Cues, comments and video feedback, all in the MEO app.

Every 4 weeks

Building toward what you train for

From there it builds toward what your training actually asks of you, heavier and faster and closer to the demands of your sport. There's regular testing throughout each phase, so you earn the right to progress.
