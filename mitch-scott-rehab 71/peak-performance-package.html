HOW IT WORKS

What the 28 days look like.

Week 1

Assessment, finding your starting point

60 minutes in-person, or online. We'll go through where you're at, what you've already tried, and start working back from your end goal.

Your plan

Rehab + Training

Not just rehab. Things you can't do get modified, not missed. All delivered via app.

In between sessions

You film your exercises

How you do the exercises matters, and movement quality is a key part of rehab in my experience. I'll give you feedback so your execution improves every session.

Monday to Friday

I'm only a message away

Full support via WhatsApp between sessions, so your question doesn't have to wait a week (or longer).

As you're ready

Building towards your end goal

Heavier, faster, and more specific as you clear the phases. Your progression gets tracked and nothing gets missed.
