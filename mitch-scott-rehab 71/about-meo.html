The pivot

MEO was built for one type of person.

The person who trains before work. Who has a race on the calendar they're not willing to scratch. Who knows exactly what their body is capable of and refuses to accept that injury is the end of that story.

The system was built for the average patient, the person who just wants less pain and is happy to rest until it settles. That's not you. And pain-free isn't the finish line. Most rehab stops the moment pain does, so you come back, and three sessions in, it flares. Not because your body is broken. Because nobody built you back up to what your sport actually asks of you.

We don't just rehab the injury. We rebuild what the injury took... your capacity, your confidence, and your ability to move without second-guessing every step.

The man behind MEO

Hi, I'm Mitch.

Physiotherapist

Standard rehab approaches often prioritise high volume appointments, but shorter timeframes make it harder to get to the bottom of complex issues. I built MEO to do the opposite. To slow down, find the source, and walk beside you every step of the way until you're back doing what you love, without hesitation or fear. The devil is in the details, and the best plan in the world is worthless if it doesn't work for the person doing it. MEO exists to build every plan from the ground up, without the rush, to keep you moving forward.

I graduated from the University of Queensland with a Masters of Physiotherapy Studies, following a Bachelor of Clinical Exercise Physiology (1st Class Honours). Since then I've worked with weekend warriors, gym goers, and everyday people who simply love to train, alongside athletes from QAFL and NPL clubs. My background in Exercise Physiology helps me blend traditional physiotherapy with a refined exercise toolkit, so you get a well-rounded plan. The priority is always the same: to guide you through rehab safely and efficiently, rebuild real confidence in your body, and get you back to training without overthinking every movement.

When I'm not in the clinic, you'll usually find me training myself, hunting down Brisbane's best pastries, or heading to the beach for a weekend away (when possible...).
