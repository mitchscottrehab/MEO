Momentum /mə-ˈmen-təm/ • noun

1. Mass in motion. The force a moving object carries. Once it's moving, it tends to keep moving.

2. The drive to keep going. Hard to build from a standstill, and easier to keep than to get back.

What people usually ask me in the first five minutes.

Do I need to stop training?

Usually not. If something can't stay in your program right now, we change it. We don't just cut it and tell you to wait it out.

I've already done rehab.

Yeah, most people I see have. The difference is this doesn't stop when the pain settles. We keep building toward the thing you actually train for, and we test it as we go, so you're not guessing.

What if it's not for me?

Then I'll tell you straight, and point you to someone who's a better fit. No hard feelings.

What Momentum is built on.

Training is who you are.
Don't let an injury change that.

Most rehab is built to settle pain, so the default is to stop training. Momentum is built the other way round. Your training stays in the plan while the injury settles, and the plan keeps building once it has, toward what your training actually asks of you.

Momentum terms and conditions

These are the terms you agree to when you sign your Momentum agreement. Last updated 27 November 2026.

1. How long it runs

Momentum runs for 12 weeks, in three 4-week blocks, starting on the date of your assessment, which is your first Momentum session. You're agreeing to the full 12 weeks.

2. Price and payment

Your price is the one you chose in your agreement: Fortnightly Sessions, $2,880 for 12 weeks or 3 payments of $960; or Weekly Sessions, $3,840 for 12 weeks or 3 payments of $1,280. It's locked in for the 12 weeks. Payments are invoiced through Cliniko. If you pay in 3 payments, the first is due before your assessment and the next two are due every 4 weeks after that. Card surcharges are already included in the price.

3. Your assessment

Your assessment is your first Momentum session and is included in the price. If at your assessment I find Momentum isn't the right plan for you, I'll tell you and make sure you're placed in the hands of someone who can help. In that case you pay for the assessment only ($220), the agreement ends, and anything else you've paid is refunded.

4. Booking and changing sessions

We'll book your sessions in advance. To move one, let me know at least 24 hours before. A session cancelled with less notice, or missed, counts as used. If I need to move a session, I'll rebook it and it won't count as used.

5. Stopping early

Because Momentum is a 12-week commitment, if you stop before the 12 weeks are up, the rest of the 12-week price is still owed, apart from these cases:

  • If I decide Momentum isn't right for you. If at any point I believe it's no longer the right plan for you (for example, you need a surgical opinion or a different kind of care), I'll tell you and make sure you're placed in the hands of someone who can help. No further payments are due, and if you paid upfront, I'll refund any 4-week block we haven't started.
  • Serious illness or injury. If an illness or injury (not the one we're working on) stops you taking part, we can discuss the option to pause or cancel the remainder of the agreement.

6. After the 12 weeks

Momentum continues every 4 weeks at the same rate ($960 every 4 weeks for Fortnightly Sessions, or $1,280 every 4 weeks for Weekly Sessions) unless you tell me otherwise. Around week 10 we'll review where you're at and decide together whether to carry on, change how often we meet, or what a discharge plan might look like. After the 12 weeks you can stop or change frequency at any time, with no fee, by telling me before your next payment is due.

7. How to stop or change

Tell me in writing: email mitch@meorehabperformance.com or message me in the MEO app. I'll confirm in writing within 2 business days.

8. What I can and can't promise

I'll assess you properly, explain what we're working on and why, and keep the plan moving with you. I can't tell you what your body will do in the next few months, and nobody honest can. There's no guaranteed outcome.

9. Your rights

Nothing in this agreement takes away your rights under the Australian Consumer Law, including the consumer guarantees.

10. Your information

Your clinical records are kept in Cliniko. Your program, messages and videos are kept in the MEO app. App information is stored in Australia, and videos are stored in Australia where possible. How I handle your information is in my Privacy Policy. My general Terms & Conditions also apply.
